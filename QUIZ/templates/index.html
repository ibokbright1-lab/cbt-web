How This Round Works (Fair Play & Rules)

  • Class-Matched Questions: You will answer questions strictly from your own class syllabus so JSS 1 – SSS 3 compete fairly.
  • Separate Device Rule: Every participant must compete independently on their own signed-in device.
  • Speed Tie-Breaker: High accuracy + faster finish earns bonus points to break ties on the World Cup bracket.
  • One Official Attempt: Each member gets one official submission per stage. Switching tabs triggers integrity warnings.
